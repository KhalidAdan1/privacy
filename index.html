Privacy Policy

COLNIGHTD LTD

Effective date: 25 May 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how COLNIGHTD LTD collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you visit our website, contact us, or use services operated by COLNIGHTD LTD, including apps such as Better Nights and Idara. Idara helps Qur'an teachers, students, parents, and masjids or organisations manage lessons, assignments, memorisation progress, feedback, attendance, classes, subscriptions, notifications, and communication.

1. Who We Are

COLNIGHTD LTD is the data controller for personal data processed through this website and services we operate, including Idara.

General contact: support@betternightsapp.co.uk
Idara contact: contact@al-idara.com

2. What This Policy Covers

This policy applies to:

  • Visitors to the COLNIGHTD LTD website.
  • People who contact us by email.
  • Basic technical data processed to operate, secure, and maintain the website.
  • Users of Idara, including teachers, students, parents or guardians, and masjid, madrassah, academy, or organisation administrators.

Our apps and services may have their own privacy notices where they process app-specific data. Those notices should be read together with this policy where relevant.

3. Information We Collect

  • Contact data: your email address, name if provided, and message content when you contact us.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, timestamps, and server logs.
  • Usage data: limited website usage information if analytics or hosting tools are used.
  • Idara account data: names, email addresses, user roles, account identifiers, teacher codes, parent-child links, masjid or organisation links, and account settings.
  • Learning data: assignments, Qur'an surah and ayah progress, custom assignment details, ratings, teacher feedback, lesson dates and times, attendance records, class information, meeting links or manually entered location text, and progress records.
  • Communication data: chat messages, sender details, timestamps, read status, and linked parent visibility information.
  • Subscription and notification data: subscription status, plan information, payment provider references, push notification tokens, and device notification details.

4. How We Use Information

  • Respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • Operate, maintain, and secure the website.
  • Understand basic website performance and reliability.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights.
  • Provide Idara teacher, student, parent, and organisation features.
  • Link students with teachers, parents or guardians, classes, and organisations.
  • Manage lessons, assignments, feedback, attendance, progress, reminders, and notifications.
  • Process subscriptions and control access to paid or organisation features.
  • Support safeguarding, parent visibility, account security, and misuse prevention.

5. Legal Bases

Under UK GDPR, we process personal data where one or more of these legal bases apply:

  • Legitimate interests: to operate, secure, improve, and respond through the website and our services.
  • Contract: to provide paid or requested services, including Idara accounts, subscriptions, and teaching features.
  • Consent: where required for optional cookies, analytics, marketing, or child-related processing where consent is the appropriate basis.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is required by law.
  • Child-related processing: where appropriate, we rely on parent or guardian involvement, or the authority of a teacher, masjid, madrassah, academy, or organisation responsible for the child's learning.

6. Sharing and Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers such as website hosting, email, analytics, or security providers. For Idara, this may include Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, database, cloud functions, notifications, and infrastructure; RevenueCat for subscription management; Stripe for web payments; Apple App Store and Google Play for app subscriptions and payments; Expo for push notifications; Vercel for website hosting; and email or support tools where needed to respond to support requests. They may process personal data only as needed to provide those services.

We do not sell personal data.

7. International Transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as adequacy regulations, standard contractual clauses, UK transfer mechanisms, and provider data protection terms.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, operating the website, maintaining security, providing Idara services, maintaining learning records, handling account recovery, resolving disputes, supporting safeguarding, and meeting legal obligations.

9. Your Rights

Subject to legal limits, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Request deletion of personal data.
  • Restrict or object to processing.
  • Request data portability.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or another relevant authority.

To exercise your rights, contact support@betternightsapp.co.uk or, for Idara, contact@al-idara.com.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls, secure hosting practices, authentication, database security rules, restricted administrative access, and encrypted connections where available. No online service can guarantee complete security.

11. Children

Some COLNIGHTD LTD services, including Idara, may be used by children in an educational and religious learning context. Students under 15 should have a linked parent or guardian account before using core student features. Children under 13 must not create or use an account unless this has been approved by a parent, guardian, teacher, masjid, madrassah, academy, or organisation responsible for their learning.

Linked parents or guardians may be able to view a student's assignments, progress, feedback, lessons, attendance, and messages between the student and teacher for safeguarding, learning oversight, and support. Students should not treat student-teacher chat as private from a linked parent or guardian. We do not sell children's personal data or knowingly use children's data for advertising profiling.
